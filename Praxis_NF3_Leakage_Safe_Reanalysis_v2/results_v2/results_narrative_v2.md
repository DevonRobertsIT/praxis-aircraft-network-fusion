# Notebook 07 Results Narrative

## Dataset and evaluation design

The consolidated analysis retained 12,185 candidate low-altitude point events. Model comparisons used 1,828 common held-out events so all paired AUPRC differences were calculated on identical targets. The cyber labels and features originate from the controlled Notebook 03 aircraft-to-NF3 temporal mapping; they do not represent observed site-coincident attacks at PANC.

## RQ1 and H1

Processing-time timestamps had an MAE of 1.372 seconds relative to aircraft-reported event time. Event-time timestamps had an MAE of 0 seconds by definition, yielding a reduction of 1.372 seconds (95% bootstrap CI 1.327 to 1.421). H1 was supported under the preregistered 1.0-second criterion. This result evaluates timestamp fidelity, not real cross-domain clock synchronization.

## Model comparison

The strongest held-out point estimate was produced by **Fused tabular — decision_tree** with test AUPRC 1.0000. The full R-GCN minus Transformer AUPRC difference was -0.0821; H2 was not supported. The full R-GCN minus lag/relation-ablation difference was +0.0119, and the full R-GCN local p95 latency was 2.410 ms; H3 was not supported.

## Sensitivity and explainability

Window sensitivity was summarized for every Notebook 03 temporal window without retraining the models. Subgroup diagnostics retained frozen validation-selected thresholds and omitted groups that were too small or lacked both classes. Transformer and R-GCN attribution magnitudes were ranked within their own explanation methods and were not treated as directly comparable effect sizes.

## Interpretation boundary

The findings characterize predictive and explanatory behavior under a controlled multimodal alignment experiment. They do not show that any NF3 flow caused, accompanied, or was generated by a specific aircraft event.
